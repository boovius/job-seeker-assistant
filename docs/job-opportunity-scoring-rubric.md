# Job Opportunity Scoring Rubric

## Purpose
This rubric is meant to help Josh evaluate future climate job opportunities more consistently.

The goal is not to automate judgment away. The goal is to create a structured, repeatable filter so opportunities can be compared against the same underlying criteria instead of being judged from scratch every time.

## Scoring scale
Use a 1–5 scale for each dimension:

- **1** = very weak fit
- **2** = weak fit
- **3** = mixed / uncertain
- **4** = strong fit
- **5** = excellent fit

## Core dimensions

### 1. Climate mission alignment (20%)
How strongly does the company/role align with the climate work Josh actually cares about?

High score:
- directly climate relevant
- mission feels real and meaningful
- work feels worth spending serious time on

### 2. Role-shape fit (25%)
How well does the role match Josh’s sweet spot?

High score:
- technical product role
- product-heavy engineering role
- systems-oriented cross-functional role
- not too generic PM, not too siloed engineering, not too vague ops

### 3. Systems / ambiguity fit (15%)
How much does the role reward synthesis, ambiguity handling, and turning fuzzy situations into structure?

High score:
- messy problem space
- need for systems thinking
- cross-functional bridge work
- real clarity-making required

### 4. Technical leverage (15%)
How much does Josh’s technical fluency matter in this role?

High score:
- technical understanding is useful and respected
- ability to reason about implementation and systems matters
- role is not purely adjacent to technical work

### 5. Product / strategic influence (10%)
How much influence is there over product shape, prioritization, or strategic direction?

High score:
- room to shape direction, not just execute tickets
- product thinking and tradeoff judgment matter

### 6. Team / stage fit (10%)
How well does the company stage and team context fit Josh’s working style?

High score:
- right level of ownership
- right level of ambiguity
- generalist/system-builder strengths are useful

### 7. Practical viability (5%)
Is this actually a realistic and worthwhile opportunity to pursue now?

High score:
- role is open
- realistic fit
- location/comp/timing not obviously broken
- no immediate red flags

## Weighted interpretation
Suggested weighted score bands:

- **85–100** → strong pursue
- **70–84** → worth considering
- **55–69** → maybe / context-dependent
- **below 55** → low priority

## How to use
For each opportunity:
1. score each dimension 1–5
2. apply the weighting
3. total the score
4. write one sentence explaining the result

The explanation sentence matters because it preserves judgment and nuance.

### Example explanation
> Strong climate alignment and excellent systems fit, but only moderate role-shape fit because it leans too far toward pure operations.

## Working assumption behind this rubric
This rubric reflects the current understanding that Josh’s strongest fit is likely in climate roles that combine some mix of:
- technical product thinking
- product-heavy engineering or technical building
- systems-oriented cross-functional leverage

It is meant to evolve as the search evolves.
